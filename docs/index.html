The same imagery, made explicit
The same five images rewritten as named actions. Line three runs 9.51 m where the poem above managed 0.38 m.
Nothing here generates motion. That needs a GPU and three model workers running on your own machine, and this is a static site. What does work, with nothing installed: opening a saved session and watching every body in it, scrubbing it, reading the notation, and rating it. The motions are inside the files.

Press Generate or Bake and the instrument will say the service is absent rather than pretending otherwise. It does not go looking for one, either — a page on the open web reaching for something on your machine is what makes a browser stop and ask permission, and the answer here would be "nothing there" regardless.

Running the stack yourself? Add ?service to the instrument's address and it will connect to localhost:8000.
